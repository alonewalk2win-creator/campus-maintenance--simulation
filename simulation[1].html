Model: same random complaints for both systems. Manual register: the clerk takes complaints in arrival order, picks any technician of the trade without seeing their load (and does not know who is on leave), some complaints are never written down, and about one in three closures has no remarks. Database system: priority order, least-loaded technician within capacity, jobs of a technician on leave return to the queue, and rule violations are blocked. Both dispatchers handle at most 3 complaints per hour. A technician carrying more jobs than the maximum works slower on each job.
